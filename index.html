How to Make Scrambled Eggs

  1. Gather the ingredients
    1. 2 eggs
    2. Salt
    3. Pepper
    4. Butter
  2. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
  3. Beat the eggs until they are mixed well.
  4. Heat a pan and add a small amount of butter.
  5. Pour the eggs into the pan.
  6. Stir the eggs gently until they are fully cooked.
  7. Serve the scrambled eggs while they are hot.
